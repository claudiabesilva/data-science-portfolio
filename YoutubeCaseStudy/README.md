# YouTube Trending Video Analysis

End-to-end analysis of YouTube trending data combining **comment sentiment**, **emoji patterns**, and **engagement metrics** across 10 countries (~340K videos).

## Overview

This project is split into two parts:

1. **Comment Analysis** — sentiment scoring, word clouds, and emoji frequency on US YouTube comments
2. **Multi-country Video Analysis** — engagement patterns, category rankings, channel trends, and title punctuation impact across 10 countries

## Key Findings

- The YouTube audience skews positive — the top emojis are 😂 (36,987), 😍 (33,453), and ❤️ (16,911)
- **Music** is the most liked category by a wide margin (~5.8B total likes vs. ~2.4B for Entertainment)
- Views and likes have a strong linear relationship (correlation = **0.78**) — a 1% increase in views predicts ~0.78% increase in likes
- **The Late Show with Stephen Colbert** had the most trending videos (710), followed by WWE (643) and Late Night with Seth Meyers (592)

## Analysis Breakdown

### Part 1 — Comment Sentiment & Emoji Analysis
- Loaded `UScomments.csv` and dropped 26 rows with missing comment text
- Applied **NLTK VADER** sentiment scoring to a sample of 1,000 comments
  - Compound score ranges from -1 (most negative) to +1 (most positive)
  - Filtered strong positives (≥ 0.8) and strong negatives (≤ -0.8)
- Generated **word clouds** for each sentiment group using WordCloud + STOPWORDS
- Extracted and counted all emojis from the full dataset using the `emoji` library + `Counter`

### Part 2 — Multi-country Video Analysis
- Loaded 10 country CSVs (375,942 rows), removed 36,417 duplicates → **339,525 clean rows**
- Exported combined dataset to CSV, JSON, and SQLite using SQLAlchemy
- Mapped numeric `category_id` to human-readable names using the US category JSON
- Computed engagement rates per view: `like_rate`, `dislike_rate`, `comment_count_rate`
- Calculated correlation matrix for likes, dislikes, and views
- Ranked channels by number of trending video appearances
- Counted punctuation characters in video titles and compared against views and likes
